# Issue #759 Phase D — renderer-independent gap repair

## Scope

Phase D moves repaired-batch settlement entirely into the TimelineActor relay/display-projection path. `GapProjectionRelayed` records the actor, timeline, repair, and minimum-batch fence after the causal projection accepts the exact tagged batch. The actor publishes `TimelineGapRepairProgressed` and then continues bounded inspection without waiting for WebView layout.

- `AcknowledgeTimelineBatchRendered` Core/account/manager/actor messages;

- App/TimelineView render acknowledgement state and submission;
- `timelineAcknowledgementDelivery` and retry/backoff tests;
- `awaiting_projection`, render-settlement timeout/task/recovery, and obsolete QA ACK injection.

Viewport observations and local layout/virtualization behavior remain presentation and prioritization facts only.
